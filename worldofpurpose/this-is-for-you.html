This Is For You If…

Is For You

You feel called to something bigger — but every time you try to name it, the words fall short and the feeling fades

You've achieved things on the outside — the job, the title, the life that looks right — and still feel empty on the inside

You know God placed something in you — but fear, doubt, or the opinions of others have kept you from fully walking in it

You're tired of living someone else's definition of success and ready to discover what yours actually looks like

You're ready to do the inner work — not just read about it, but actually go there and let something shift

Not For You

You're looking for a quick fix or a formula that tells you exactly what to do without doing any inner work

You think clarity about your purpose is something that just happens to you — not something you actively step into

You want someone to validate the life you've already been living — not challenge you to look deeper at what's underneath it

You're still betting that more hustle, more strategy, or the next program will give you the direction you're missing

You're not willing to question the identity and beliefs that have been running your life up until now
